# CivicGuard Exercise Privacy Policy

Effective: 19 October 2026

This policy covers the packaged CivicGuard Exercise application for iPhone and iPad. It also describes the same offline exercise when packaged for other platforms. The development website has different network behavior, described below.

## Data stored on your device

CivicGuard saves the exercise drafts you edit in English, Spanish and Chinese, your selected language, and your simulated channel choices. Native mobile apps use operating-system preferences; browser and desktop versions use local web storage. Review approval is not stored. The developer does not automatically receive your drafts or operate a server that stores them.

Saved edits remain until you reset drafts, clear app or browser data, or remove the app. Your operating system may include app data in backups or device transfers according to your system settings. CivicGuard does not provide its own cloud backup or synchronization. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into exercise drafts.

## Packaged app network behavior

The packaged app includes its exercise data, map and interface and makes no automatic external network requests. It includes no advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. No account is required.

## Optional sharing and external links

Sharing starts only when you choose **Share exercise summary**. A summary includes all three language drafts and selected simulated channels, with a clear simulation label. Your operating system and the destination you choose handle this transfer under their own privacy practices. CivicGuard does not upload an additional copy to a developer-operated server. You control copies you save or share at their destinations.

Support and official-information links open an external website in your browser. These websites receive normal request information and apply their own privacy practices. Support is provided through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/ii300/CivicGuard/issues). If you submit an issue, its contents and your GitHub profile are public. Do not include personal information or sensitive exercise drafts. GitHub’s [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) explains its handling of this information.

## Deleting your data

Choose **Reset drafts**, then **Reset all drafts**, to remove saved exercise edits on this device and restore the original exercise text and channel choices. This does not erase copies already in a system backup or copies you have exported. Delete shared or exported copies through the destination app or service. Manage device backups through your operating-system settings.

## Development website

The Flask/development website is not the offline App Store package. It can request interface files from unpkg and map images and public data from Esri/ArcGIS. These services receive normal request information, such as IP address, browser information and the displayed map area. Draft editing does not automatically upload your text to those map services. Optional development backend features are outside the packaged app.

## Questions and updates

Contact the maintainer through the public [CivicGuard support channel](https://github.com/ii300/CivicGuard/issues) for privacy or support questions. This channel is not an emergency service. Changes to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date.
