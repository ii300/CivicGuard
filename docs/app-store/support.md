# CivicGuard Exercise Support

CivicGuard Exercise is an offline tabletop tool for practicing wildfire message review. It uses an illustrative historical scenario and does not send public alerts or provide current evacuation instructions.

## Contact the maintainer

[Open the CivicGuard support channel on GitHub](https://github.com/ii300/CivicGuard/issues). You can browse existing reports without signing in; submitting an issue may require a GitHub account.

Include your app version, device model, operating-system version, what you were trying to do, and the steps that caused the problem. Issues are public. Do not post personal details or sensitive draft text. Screenshots should hide personal information.

This support channel does not provide emergency assistance. During a real incident, follow verified local authority information.

## Common questions

**Does this app require an internet connection?**

The packaged exercise, draft editing, saved drafts and help work offline. Support and other external links require a connection. Sharing depends on the destination you choose.

**Where are my drafts?**

Drafts and simulated channel choices are saved on this device. Wait for **Saved on this device** before closing. The app does not sync drafts between installations; operating-system backup and transfer settings may still apply.

**Why is review pending after I reopen the app?**

Review approval is deliberately not stored. Recheck the draft before choosing **Approve Simulation** again. Editing a draft, changing language or changing channels also requires a fresh review.

**How do I clear saved drafts?**

Select **Reset drafts**, then **Reset all drafts**. This clears your saved edits and restores the original exercise. Shared or exported files and older system backups must be managed separately.

**What does sharing send?**

The summary includes all three language drafts and the selected simulated channels. It always identifies the content as a simulation. Inspect every language before sharing. Opening the share options does not authorize an actual public alert.

**The app says it could not save. What should I do?**

Keep the app open, preserve your work through sharing or an appropriate local destination, and check device storage. If reading previously saved drafts failed, autosave stays paused to protect that unread copy. Current edits remain only in the open window: share or copy them before reopening to retry reading. Choosing **Reset drafts** and confirming **Reset all drafts** explicitly replaces the saved copy and resumes autosave after clearing succeeds. Canceling or a failed reset leaves that copy protected.

If drafts were read successfully but a later write fails, editing again retries saving. Each language has a 20,000 UTF-16 code-unit limit, and the saved record has a 200 KiB total limit. If the problem continues, report the visible error and steps through the support channel.
